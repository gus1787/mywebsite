The experience

Make the choosing part feel like part of the art.

Pet Portrait starts with two-to-four pet photos, then uses a Tinder-style deck to let someone shape the tone and artistic direction before a portrait is generated. The app protects the unwatermarked original behind the unlock flow while making the preview moment useful on its own.

01

Show the pet

Photo intake includes validation, private upload behavior, resumable upload handling, and deletion boundaries in the MVP design.

02

Find the feeling

Like/pass swipes create a versioned, reproducible taste profile instead of asking for a long style questionnaire.

03

Preview with care

Generation state, watermarked preview delivery, unlock verification, and library flows are designed around a protected original.

Review-safe status

The flow is shaped; the release environment is not.

The current browser harness uses fictional deterministic data. It does not call Firebase, OpenAI, Apple payments, or a production service, and the native app still needs environment and device validation.
