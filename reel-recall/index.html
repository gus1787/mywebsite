The idea

Small rounds, useful memory, a little more fun.

Reel Recall asks focused questions about familiar shows: who played the character, what the character is called, and which other roles an actor has played. The verified prototype supports three sample shows, multiple question modes, swipe and keyboard input, XP, streaks, and round summaries.

01

Solo Learning

The current practice mode is designed for a fast five-question session with immediate feedback and a review round for missed cards.

02

Choose your tone

Arcade, Campus, and Everyday concepts explore different ways to position the same learning loop for competitive, editorial, or casual audiences.

03

Time Rush, later

Live friend races, durable results, leaderboards, and telemetry remain planned product work—not features this preview promises.
