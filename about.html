Introduction

Hi, I'm Gus Phillips, I believe that my background and journey to where I am today brings a unique perspective to problem solving in any organization.

My Journey

I grew up as the oldest of four brothers, and I've always had to be a leader and quick -- yet accurate -- decision maker.

I grew up on the computer, and have been tinkering and building computers since I was 9. I remember buying a stick of ram that had 512 mb to double the speed of my computer.

Around 16, I had a job working in a restaurant doing cashier work. When we were slow, I would bring up my laptop and see if I could get Counter-Strike to work on the monitor at the front of house.

At that job I also learned how to program their POS(Point of Sale) machine to add new items, and adjust prices.

While in college I played collegiate league of legends, and while I didn't have the highest level of skill, I was able to communicate effectively and lead the team from my role.

After college, I took a quick detour to China to teach English and wait for my Spanish Program to begin. I ended up living in Jining, Shandong Province for 4 months, and then lived in Spain for 2 years, up until Summer 2019. Upon returning to the States, I got a job at Lockheed Martin, and worked there until I graduated from the University of Texas at Dallas, where I received my Master's in Business Administration.

Once I received my Masters, I left Lockheed Martin for Northrop Grumman. Where I began work as a Data Management Analyst, and moved quickly into work as a Data Engineer.

Currently, I work at Northrop Grumman doing work for Facilities Analytics.

Personal Interests

Outside of work, I enjoy travelling, trying new restaurants, and staying active. I'm also involved in a Chess Club, and a Spanish club weekly. I'm getting married in the Fall, and I've moved across country twice in the last two years.
